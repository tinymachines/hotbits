Statistical Test Failure Rates Over Time

This chart shows the failure rates of various statistical randomness tests over time. Lower failure rates indicate better randomness quality. The tests include measures for entropy, distribution uniformity, and independence of generated numbers.

P-Values Distribution Over Time

This chart displays the distribution of p-values from statistical tests over time. Healthy random number generators should show uniform p-value distributions. Clustering or patterns in p-values can indicate bias or predictability in the random output.

Analysis Information

Test Suite: These reports are generated using industry-standard statistical test suites including NIST STS (Statistical Test Suite) and Dieharder tests.

Update Frequency: Reports are updated automatically as new entropy data is processed and tested.

Quality Assurance: The Hotbits system continuously monitors entropy quality to ensure cryptographic-grade randomness. Any significant deviation from expected statistical properties triggers alerts and investigation.
